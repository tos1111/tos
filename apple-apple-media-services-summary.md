Apple provides parental controls and age-gating mechanisms to protect minors, including requiring parental or guardian creation of accounts for children under 13. <sup>[65-65](https://github.com/tos1111/tos/blob/main/apple%2FApple%20Media%20Services%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.txt#L65-L65 "Apple Accounts for persons under this age can be created by a parent or legal guardian using Family Sharing")</sup>

Apple automatically enables parental purchase approval for young children by default, protecting minors and parents from unauthorized or accidental in-app purchases. <sup>[193-193](https://github.com/tos1111/tos/blob/main/apple%2FApple%20Media%20Services%20Terms%20and%20Conditions.txt#L193-L193 "Ask to Buy is enabled by default for any Family member under the age of thirteen")</sup>
